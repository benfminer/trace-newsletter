— About these newsletters

Written for TRACE, not for a filing cabinet.

Both publications follow the same rule: one topic an issue, and everything in it has to be usable with a student that week. Where a claim rests on research, it says so. Where the evidence is thin or contested, it says that too, because a practice you can defend is worth more than one you merely repeat.

Each issue also carries at least one working tool rather than a description of one — a model you can click through, a checker you can run on a real caseload, a tracker that remembers your week. Nothing is sent anywhere; the trackers save to your own device.

Ten issues, August
through May.

Both runs are complete. Transition Matters went from Indicator 14 as a scoreboard in August to Indicator 14 as feedback in May, by way of assessment, IEP quality, self-determination, paid work, job retention, career planning, family, and the agency handoff. Side by Side went from the prompting ladder to the fall para’s first week, and spent the year on the part of the job that happens with a student rather than about one.

Two research-based predictors never got their issue — inclusion in general education, and self-care and independent living skills. That is worth saying out loud rather than letting the run imply it covered everything. Every issue stays here, and the interactive tools keep working.
